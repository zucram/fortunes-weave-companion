What is Fortune's Weave?

Fire Emblem's 18th mainline entry, out now on Nintendo Switch 2 (Sept 17, 2026). You play Eshmel, the Herald of the Pale Raven — a customizable avatar sent back in time from the demon god Balor's ruined future to save the four Heroes of the Dagdan Empire. The story spans two eras: the Heroic Games in the city of Dagsion (~year 1449), and a timeskip to 1454 when the war with Balor begins.

The four Heroes

Cai — enter the Games to free his imprisoned father, Castor
Dietrich — Fódlan noble, shipwrecked; craves strong foes. Wields the relic Answerer
Theodora — queen of Saramis, sworn to restore the goddess Yu Phas
Leda — dancer-musician hunting her father's killer, the White-Maned Demon Bertrand

Read this first

One autosave only. There is no manual save-slot management. Fortuna's Blessing (turn rewind) is your safety net — don't treat rewinds as failures.
Heroes run in parallel. You can time-travel between Hero paths; progress saves per Hero. Finish one route's loop before seriously pushing another.
Deadlines move. Quest and qualifier deadlines can differ per route. Check the in-game calendar every Free Time block.

Always useful

Small decision flows that apply across the whole game.

Blaze Arts discipline

Hero-only super moves: cost HP + the Blaze gauge
↓
Overuse halves max HP for the rest of the battle
↓
Save them for: boss kills, swarm clears, map-swing moments

Fortuna's Blessing

Rewind to any earlier point after a move
↓
Rewind cheaply, early — fix bad positioning immediately
↓
If you're rewinding constantly, the plan is wrong, not the execution

Weapon durability

Combat Arts burn durability — budget them
↓
Enhance/refine at the Shopping Arcade with dungeon materials
↓
Unique weapons need dual proficiencies (e.g. Veroquine: B Sword + C Axe) and carry a Curse stat

Dungeon squad (Clashes)
